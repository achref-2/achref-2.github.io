Pick a letter and see what Amt-Lotse shows for it: first what to do and by when, then the facts it checked, a plain explanation, the read-aloud audio and a reply draft.

View only. These are real, unedited results that Amt-Lotse produced on my computer. All letters are made up for testing. The AI does not run on this page: the real tool runs locally, so no letter ever leaves the user's computer.
